Multicellular control of bacterial populations

Feedback is central to biological regulation and engineered systems. Single-cell feedback controllers are powerful but face limitations due to resource competition and chemical crosstalk. My research develops strategies to distribute control across multiple interacting populations — enabling cooperative controllers that scale and are more robust in practice.
